Font for inserted text

Match this to your usual Outlook font, so templates don't stand out from what you type.

Subject line

When ticked, inserting a template replaces the email's subject. You can still untick it for a single email.

Storage

Templates are saved in your Outlook mailbox, which gives add-ins about 32 KB. Text compresses well, but images can't be stored.

Backup

Paste the backup into Notepad and save it as a .json file.
